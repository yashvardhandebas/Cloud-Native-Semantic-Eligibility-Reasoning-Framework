Government of India Notification: Atal Pension Yojana (APY) - PFRDA
Eligibility Criteria and Operational Guidelines:
1. Target Beneficiaries: Any Indian citizen aged between 18 and 40 years holding a valid savings bank account or post office savings bank account.
2. Contributory Pension Entitlement: Subscribers can choose a guaranteed minimum monthly pension of Rs. 1,000, Rs. 2,000, Rs. 3,000, Rs. 4,000, or Rs. 5,000 per month starting from the age of 60 years until demise, with return of pension corpus to the nominee.
3. Required Verification Documents: Aadhaar Card, Active Bank Passbook / Savings Account details, and mobile phone number for SMS alerts.
4. Statutory Exclusion Criteria:
- Income Tax Payer Exclusion: With effect from 1st October 2022, any citizen who is or has been an income tax payer under the Income Tax Act is strictly ineligible to join the Atal Pension Yojana.
- Existing beneficiaries receiving any statutory social security scheme with government co-contribution.
