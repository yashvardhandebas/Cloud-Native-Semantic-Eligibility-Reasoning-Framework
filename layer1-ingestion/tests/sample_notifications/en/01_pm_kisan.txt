Government of India Notification: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
Eligibility Criteria and Operational Guidelines:
1. All landholding farmer families who hold cultivable land in their names as per state land records are eligible for financial assistance.
2. The beneficiary family will receive an income support benefit of Rs. 6000 per annum, paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2000 every four months.
3. The monetary benefit will be transferred directly to the verified bank account of the beneficiary via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
4. Mandatory documents to be submitted include: Valid Aadhaar Card, Land ownership passbook/record (Patta/Khasra/Khatauni), and active Bank Passbook details linked with Aadhaar.
5. Ineligible categories (Exclusions):
- All institutional landholders.
- Farmer families where one or more members hold or held constitutional posts.
- Former and present Members of Parliament (Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha), Members of Legislative Assemblies or Councils.
- Former or retired officers and employees of central/state government ministries or departments.
- All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
- Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, and Chartered Accountants registered with professional bodies.
