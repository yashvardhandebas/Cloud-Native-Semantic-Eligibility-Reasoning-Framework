Government of India Notification: Sukanya Samriddhi Account Scheme (SSY) - Ministry of Finance / National Savings Institute
Eligibility Criteria and Operational Guidelines:
1. Target Beneficiary: The account can be opened by natural or legal guardians in the name of an Indian girl child from her birth until she attains the age of 10 years.
2. Account Limits: A guardian can open only one account in the name of one girl child, and a maximum of two accounts in the name of two girl children in one family (with exceptions allowed for twins or triplets born in the first/second order).
3. Savings and Interest Benefits: Sovereign backed high-interest small savings deposit offering 8.2% per annum compound interest, completely exempt from income tax under Section 80C and Section 10(10D) (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt status).
4. Required Documents: Birth Certificate of the girl child (age proof), Aadhaar Card of parent or legal guardian, Proof of Residence, and recent passport-size Photographs.
5. Ineligible Conditions (Exclusions):
- Opening of a third account in a family where no twin/triplet birth certificate is furnished.
- Girl children who have already attained the age of 10 years on the date of account opening.
