Government of India Notification: Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)
Eligibility Criteria and Operational Guidelines:
1. Target beneficiaries include families identified as deprived or vulnerable under the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) rural deprivation criteria or designated urban occupational categories.
2. Senior Citizen Universal Expansion: Any Indian citizen aged 70 years and above is eligible for a distinct top-up cover of Rs. 5,00,000 per year on a family basis, irrespective of socio-economic status or household income.
3. Health Benefit Entitlement: Eligible beneficiary families receive cashless and paperless health insurance coverage of up to Rs. 5,00,000 per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across empaneled public and private hospitals.
4. Mandatory Verification Documents: Valid Aadhaar Card (mandatory for identity and age proof e-KYC), Ration Card or Family Composite ID, and Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Card.
5. Ineligible Categories (Exclusions):
- Beneficiary families already enrolled in and covered under other statutory central government health schemes including CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme), ECHS (Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme), or CAPF health coverage cannot claim duplicate PM-JAY benefits.
