Government of India Notification: PM Vishwakarma Scheme - Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME)
Eligibility Criteria and Operational Guidelines:
1. Target Beneficiary: An artisan or craftsperson working with hands and traditional tools, engaged in one of the 18 recognized traditional trades (such as Carpenter, Boat Maker, Armourer, Blacksmith, Hammer and Tool Kit Maker, Locksmith, Sculptor/Stone Carver, Goldsmith, Potter, Cobbler/Footwear Artisan, Mason/Rajmistri, Basket/Mat/Broom Maker, Doll & Toy Maker, Barber/Nai, Garland Maker, Washerman/Dhobi, Tailor/Darzi, and Fishing Net Maker) on a self-employment basis.
2. Minimum Age: The minimum age of the artisan must be 18 years on the date of registration.
3. Scheme Benefits:
- PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card recognition.
- Skill Training with a stipend of Rs. 500 per day.
- Toolkit incentive grant of Rs. 15,000 via e-RUPI / digital voucher.
- Collateral-free enterprise credit of up to Rs. 1,00,000 (Tranche 1) and Rs. 2,00,000 (Tranche 2) at a concessional interest rate of 5%.
4. Mandatory Documents: Valid Aadhaar Card, Active Bank Account Passbook, Artisan Proof/Trade declaration, and mobile number linked with Aadhaar.
5. Ineligible Criteria (Exclusions):
- Regular government employees and their immediate family members are excluded.
- Beneficiaries who have availed similar credit-based schemes for self-employment (PMEGP, PM Mudra, PM SVANidhi) and are in active default.
- The registration is restricted to one member per family.
