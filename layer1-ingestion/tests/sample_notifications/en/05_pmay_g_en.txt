Government of India Notification: Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)
Eligibility Criteria and Operational Guidelines:
1. Target beneficiaries include rural families living in houseless condition or living in zero, one or two-room kutcha houses with kutcha roof and walls as identified by the SECC 2011 deprivation index or Awaas+ permanent waitlist.
2. Financial Assistance: Beneficiaries in plain areas receive grant assistance of Rs. 1,20,000 per unit, while beneficiaries in hilly, difficult, and Integrated Action Plan (IAP) districts receive Rs. 1,30,000 per unit for construction of quality pucca houses.
3. Supplementary Entitlements: Additional assistance of Rs. 12,000 for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin (SBM-G) and 90 to 95 days of unskilled labor wages under MGNREGA.
4. Required Verification Documents: Valid Aadhaar Card, MGNREGA Job Card Number, Bank Account Passbook, and Homestead Land Allotment Document or Land Record.
5. Ineligible Categories (Exclusion Criteria):
- Households already owning a pucca house in the village or elsewhere.
- Households owning motorized two, three, or four-wheelers, or mechanized agricultural equipment.
- Households having any member as a regular government employee or public sector employee.
- Households where any member pays income tax or professional tax.
- Households with any member earning more than Rs. 15,000 per month.
