Government of India Notification: National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) - Ministry of Education
Eligibility Criteria and Operational Guidelines:
1. Target Beneficiary: Meritorious students studying as regular students in Class VIII in a government, local body, or government-aided school after clearing Class VII examination.
2. Academic Qualification Criteria: Students must have secured at least 55% marks or equivalent grade in Class VII examination (relaxable by 5% for SC and ST students, i.e., minimum 50% marks).
3. Parental Income Ceiling: The annual family income of parents from all sources must not exceed Rs. 3,50,000 per annum.
4. Selection & Scholarship Entitlement: Candidates qualifying through the state-level selection examination receive a scholarship of Rs. 12,000 per annum (Rs. 1,000 per month) for classes IX to XII deposited directly into their SBI/bank account.
5. Mandatory Verification Documents: Class VII Marksheet, Income Certificate issued by competent revenue authority, Caste Certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar Card, and Bank Passbook.
6. Statutory Exclusions:
- Students studying in Kendriya Vidyalayas (KVS), Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNV), Sainik Schools, and private unaided schools are not eligible.
- Students studying in residential schools run by State Governments where boarding, lodging and education are provided free of cost.
