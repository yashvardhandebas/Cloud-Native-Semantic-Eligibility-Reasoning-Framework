Social Security Welfare Department Notification: Destitute Widow Pension Scheme
Terms and Conditions of Eligibility:
1. The applicant must be a legally widowed woman residing in the state for not less than 5 years.
2. The minimum age of the widow applicant must be 18 years and maximum age must not exceed 65 years.
3. The annual income of the applicant must not exceed Rs. 1,00,000 in rural areas and Rs. 1,20,000 in urban municipal areas.
4. Eligible widows shall be granted a monthly social security pension amount of Rs. 1500 directly into their savings bank account.
5. Mandatory documents required:
- Death Certificate of husband issued by the Registrar of Births and Deaths.
- Residence proof / Domicile certificate.
- Income certificate from Tahsildar.
- Aadhaar Card.
- Recent passport size photograph.
6. Exclusion: A widow who has remarried or whose adult children are government servants or income tax payers will not be eligible to receive this pension.
